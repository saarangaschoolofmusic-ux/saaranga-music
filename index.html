Classical Vocal Curriculum

A rigorous and traditional progression from foundational exercises to concert-level creative performance.

1. Abhyasa Ganam

Foundation building through Sarali, Jantai, Alankaram, and Geetham in 3 speeds for pitch alignment and breath control.

  • Swarasthana Precision
  • Rhythmic Discipline

2. Varnams & Swarajathis

Master Tana Varnams across multiple Talams to develop stamina, speed, and subtle Gamaka oscilations.

  • Adi & Ata Talam Varnams
  • Vocal Flexibility

3. Sabha Ganam & Kritis

Classical concert repertoire including compositions of the Trinity (Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri).

  • Classical Masterpieces
  • Sahitya Diction & Bhava

4. Manodharma Sangeetham

Advanced creative singing including Raga Alapana, Niraval, Kalpanaswaras, and Ragam Tanam Pallavi (RTP).

  • Raga Alapana & Niraval
  • RTP & Swaraprastara
