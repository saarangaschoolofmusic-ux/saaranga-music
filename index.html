Traditional Learning Pathway

Carnatic Vocal Curriculum

Structured, authentic progression from fundamental vocal warmups to concert-level creative improvisation.

1. Abhyasa Ganam

Foundation building through Sarali, Jantai, Alankaram, and Geetham in 3 speeds for rock-solid pitch alignment (Shruti Suddham).

  • Swarasthana Precision
  • Rhythm & Speed Control

2. Varnams & Swarajathis

Master Tana Varnams across multiple Talams to develop stamina, range, and subtle microtonal Gamaka oscillations.

  • Adi & Ata Talam Varnams
  • Voice Culture & Stamina

3. Sabha Ganam & Kritis

Classical concert repertoire including revered compositions of the Trinity (Tyagaraja, Muthuswami Dikshitar, Syama Sastri).

  • Classical Masterpieces
  • Sahitya Diction & Bhava

4. Manodharma

Advanced creative singing including Raga Alapana, Niraval, Kalpanaswaras, and Ragam Tanam Pallavi (RTP).

  • Raga Alapana & Niraval
  • RTP & Swaraprastara
