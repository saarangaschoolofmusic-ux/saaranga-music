Welcome to Saaranga

At Saaranga School of Music, we provide structured, comprehensive instruction in South Indian Carnatic vocal music. Guided by traditional pedagogy, our curriculum nurtures students through foundational exercises, classic Geethams, intricate Varnams, and timeless Krithis of master composers.
